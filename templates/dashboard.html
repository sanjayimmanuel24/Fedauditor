Baseline-robustness diagnostic

For each patient, R is how far the global-cohort top factor stays ahead of every competitor under the local-cohort baseline. R > 0 means the top factor survives the baseline change; R < 0 means it flips. The flip rate is only the sign of this margin. The P(R > 0) column propagates uncertainty in the local cohort mean, estimated from aggregate (n, mean, covariance) statistics.

Baseline phase diagram

The local baseline moves along bl(α) = bg + α(bl − bg). α = 0 is the global baseline and α = 1 is the observed local baseline. The flip curve is the share of patients whose leader has changed; the dashed line is the per-patient union bound, which must sit above it because the flip set is a union of half-spaces. The median critical multiplier α* is shown below the chart.
