Two baselines, two questions

Global cohort is the pooled training population across every cohort. Explaining against it asks: what makes this patient different from the whole screened population?

Local cohort is this patient's own age band. Explaining against it asks: what makes this patient different from peers of the same age?

Neither baseline is more correct. The model is unchanged; only the reference population moves.

The same weights produce different explanations depending on which population the patient is compared against.
